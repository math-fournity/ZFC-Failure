## 2. 叙事源头：Russell 1914（一手，最关键节点）

### 2.1 Russell 自己说明「解决」由什么构成

> "Zeno's arguments, in some form, have afforded grounds for almost all the theories of space
> and time and infinity which have been constructed from his day to our own."

> "Broadly speaking, the difficulties were stated by Zeno, and nothing material was added
> until we reach Bolzano's *Paradoxien des Unendlichen*, a little work written in 1847-8...
> **The definitive solution of the difficulties is due, not to Bolzano, but to Georg Cantor,
> whose work on this subject first appeared in 1882.**"

> "they are serious arguments, raising difficulties which it has taken two thousand years to
> answer, and which even now are fatal to the teachings of most philosophers."

**要点**：1914 年的「解决」被 Russell 明确定位为 **Cantor 1882**（不是 Cauchy 1821）。
这与 Z01 的零命中**完全一致**：柯西全书不提芝诺，因为按 Russell 的时间线，
芝诺的解决本来就不发生在柯西那里。

### 2.2 Russell 给出了本课题最需要的那个边界条件

> "The difficulty... is derived, I think, from the more or less unconscious operation of the
> idea of _counting_. If you set to work to count the terms in an infinite collection, you will
> never have completed your task. Thus, in the case of the runner, **if half, three-quarters,
> seven-eighths, and so on of the course were marked, and the runner was not allowed to pass
> any of the marks until the umpire said "Now," then Zeno's conclusion would be true in
> practice, and he would never reach the goal.** But it is not essential to the existence of a
> collection, or even to knowledge and reasoning concerning it, that we should be able to pass
> its terms in review one by one."

**这是 1914 年的一手文本，明文写出：加一个逐点放行条件，芝诺的结论在实践中为真。**
本 repo 的 ASK／完成门（KC-000076：对表述是否可计算、可停机的判断）在这里有一个
**1914 年的公开先例**——不是本项目的独创，也不是 20 世纪后期的产物。
（诚实边界：Russell 这句话是在为 Cantor 的集合论辩护，用「实践上会真」来划出
数学上仍可辩护的边界；他**不是**在说 Cantor 不完备。这层读法属〔我们的分析〕。）

### 2.3 Russell 对芝诺四论证的处理（与本 repo 数学行的接口）

| 芝诺论证 | Russell 1914 的处置（逐字要点） |
||---|---|
| race-course（二分） | "You cannot get to the end of a race-course"；对方的推理 "the sum of an infinite number of finite times must be infinite, and therefore the process will never be completed" 被他判为 invalid："If half the course takes half a minute, and the next quarter takes a quarter of a minute, and so on, the whole course will take a minute." |
| 论证的力量所在 | "there certainly can be something beyond the whole of an unending series" |
| 有效性判定 | 对「有限时空由有限个点/瞬间组成」这一假设，芝诺的论证 "are not sophisms, but perfectly valid" |
| 论据来源 | "we only know his arguments through Aristotle, who stated them in order to refute them" |

**接口**：Russell 最后那条判定（在有限组成假设下论证有效）与本 repo 的机器结果
（C-423 经典芝诺「极限意义到达 ∧ 没有哪一步余量为零」、C-424 圆环逐句）是
**同一数学事实的不同表述层**；本条只登记文本接口，不主张新增命题。

## 3. 建制化层：Huggett SEP（二手权威，叙事传播的公证人）

> "Following a lead given by Russell (1929, 182–198), a number of philosophers—notably
> Grünbaum (1967), but also see Salmon (2001)—took up the task of showing how modern
> mathematics could **solve** all of Zeno's paradoxes."

> "This work remains the 'received' view... [it] holds that—with certain qualifications—
> Zeno's paradoxes reveal problems that **cannot be resolved without the full resources of
> mathematics as worked out in the Nineteenth century**."

**要点**：

1. SEP 把叙事源头明确挂在 **Russell 1929**（不是 1914 讲稿；两者是不同书，见 §6 缺口 4）；
2. SEP **自己写下了一个边界**："a purely mathematical solution is not sufficient"——
   纯数学解答不足；这与本 repo F-053/C6 的方向（数学解答 ≠ 原任务完成）同形；
3. SEP 引 Huggett 原文："nothing guarantees a priori that space has the structure of the
   continuum, or even that parts of space add up according to Cauchy's definition"，
   并给出 "quantum theories of gravity likely imply that they do not"。

## 7. 一句话结论

**「极限理论解决了芝诺」不是 1821 年柯西说的话，也不是 19 世纪分析家的共识；
按 1914 年 Russell 的一手文本，它是一件 1882 年以后（Cantor）、1914 年被明确表述、
1929/1967 年被建制化的哲学判断——而 Russell 本人在同一本书里写下了它的失效条件。
教科书入口章两页零命中，是这条时间线的第三块独立证据。**
