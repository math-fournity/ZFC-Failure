## 2. Russell 如何取消「计数」这个动作（一手）

> R30：「We want, therefore, a **different method** of answering our question.」（L5679）

> R31：「In ordinary life, we decide this question by **counting**; but counting, as we saw, is
> **impossible in the case of infinite collections**, and is not logically fundamental with finite
> collections.」（L5676–5678）

然后他用**一一对应（one-one relation）**替代计数，并把**数定义为相似类之类的整体**：

> R32：「This leads us to define the number of a given collection as **the class of all collections
> that are similar to it**」（L5701–5703）

> R33（形式定义原文）：「"The number of terms in a given class" is defined as meaning "**the class of
> all classes that are similar to the given class**."」（L5706–5707）

> R34：「It is applicable **equally to finite and infinite numbers**, and it does not require the
> admission of some new and mysterious set of metaphysical entities.」（L5710–5713）

**〔我们的分析〕（单列）**：这一串动作的次序值得逐句记下：
先把「数多少」这个**动作**判定为不可执行（R30-R31），再把**结果**换成一个
不要求执行该动作的定义（R32-R33）。**动作没有被完成，动作被取消了。**

## 3. 最关键的一句：定义换成了「满足要求的对象全体」（一手）

Russell 预料到这个定义会引起不适，并给出了他的回答：

> R35：「The above definition is sure to produce, at first sight, **a feeling of oddity**, which is
> liable to cause a certain dissatisfaction.」（L5718–5719）

> R36：「**The answer to a feeling cannot be a logical argument**, but nevertheless the answer in this
> case is not without importance.」（L5723–5725）

> R37（全篇最锋利的一句）：「the real desideratum about such a definition as that of number is **not
> that it should represent as nearly as possible the ideas of those who have not gone through the
> analysis required in order to reach a definition**, but that it should **give us objects having the
> requisite properties**」（L5738–5742）

> R38：「Numbers, in fact, must satisfy the formulae of arithmetic; **any indubitable set of objects
> fulfilling this requirement may be called numbers**.」（L5742–5744）

> R39：「…In comparison with this merit, the question whether the objects to which the definition
> applies are like or unlike the vague ideas of numbers entertained by those who cannot give a
> definition, is one of **very little importance**.」（L5745–5749）

**〔我们的分析〕（单列，且这是 Z05 的核心）**：R37-R39 是**用"满足要求"替换"是什么"**的
一手范例。Russell 明说：定义不必对准未分析前那个观念，只需交出**满足算术公式的对象**；
至于这些对象像不像我们原本想的数，**不重要**。

这正是 H 深化 §2 对象层所说的机制：当"完成"的承载结构被换掉、而新对象仍满足原有公式时，
**替换在系统内不留痕迹**——所以一侧免费。差别在于：Z05 里 Russell 把这件事说得非常坦白
（"a feeling of oddity"、"The answer to a feeling cannot be a logical argument"）。
他没有隐藏代价，他只是判定代价不重要。**这与"没发现问题"不是同一回事，但它是"发现问题的人
早已把问题写下来、并主动判定它不重要"的一手证据。**

## 4. 「类是虚构」与二加一等于三（一手）

Lecture VIII 开头，Russell 处理一个针对上述定义的异议：

> R40：「There is, however, a certain logical doctrine which may be thought to form an objection to the
> above definition of numbers as classes of classes--I mean **the doctrine that there are no such
> objects as classes at all**.」（L5753–5756）

> R41：「On account of certain rather complicated difficulties, culminating in **definite
> contradictions**, I was led to the view that nothing that can be said significantly about things,
> _i.e._ particulars, can be said significantly (_i.e._ either truly or falsely) about classes of
> things.」（L5763–5766）

> R42：「**When it is admitted that classes are not things**, the question arises: What do we mean by
> statements which are nominally about classes?」（L5798–5799）

> R43（与圆环/计数 quibble 结构相邻）：「Yet **if collections of things were things, his contention
> would be irrefragable**. It is only because the bay horse and the dun cow taken together are **not a
> new thing** that we can escape the conclusion that there are three things wherever there are two.」
> （L5790–5793）

**〔我们的分析〕（单列，谨慎）**：R43 是 1914 年文本里与本 repo **圆环侧**材料结构相邻的一句：
它处理「两个东西合起来算不算第三个新东西」。Russell 的答案是：合起来**不是新东西**，
所以二加一不等于三。本 repo 的圆环讨论问的是相关的另一侧（已得到的 M 为何在反向逼近中不可复原）。
**两者不是同一命题**；本文只指出结构相邻，**不断言 Russell 处理了圆环**。

## 5. 分层纪律与禁止的推论

1. **不得**说「Russell 发现了 H」或「H 出自 1914」。归因链考古未做。
2. **不得**把 R38 读成「可以不回答过程问题」。Russell 说的是**数**可任取满足公式的对象，
   他没说任何理论都可以免付完成代价；后者是本 repo 的 H 论题，需要它自己的证明。
3. **不得**因为 R37 就写「数学界故意掩盖」。原文是坦身的自我说明（"a feeling of oddity"），
   不是掩盖。本文的用法是：**发现问题的人早已写下问题并判定其代价不重要**——
   这与"没人发现"是两件事，不得混淆。
4. **不得**据此说「Frege/Russell 的定义是错的」。它是标准数学史内容，本文不主张对错。
5. R43 与圆环的相邻性是**结构观察**，不是同一命题；若要把两者连起来，需要独立的形式化线。
